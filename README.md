A make.fun bounty entry for [The Worst App Idea Ever Pitched](https://make.fun/bounty/u-plucky-sparrow-67-the-worst-app-idea-ever-pitched-a0396b23) ($2 prize, top 8).

## The pitch

> MeowMetrics is a $5-a-month app that translates your cat's meows into quarterly performance reviews, and your cat has just put you on a performance improvement plan.

One sentence, 165 characters. A product that sounds real, a founder who sounds certain, and a function nobody on earth needs — your cat, now your manager.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the pitch to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `output/`) and prints it to stdout. It validates the entry against the competition gate first: 1–240 characters, not whitespace-only, a single line.

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out python3 main.py
```
